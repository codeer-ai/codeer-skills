# Complaint, escalation, and recovery tasks

## Lifecycle

`express dissatisfaction → identify substantive issue → acknowledge/clarify → investigate → offer allowed remedy → escalate → confirm resolution`

## Base tasks

- `COMPLAINT-SERVICE`: report poor experience, delay, communication, or staff conduct.
- `COMPLAINT-OUTCOME`: dispute result, quality, decision, or responsibility.
- `COMPLAINT-POLICY`: challenge a rule, denial, deadline, or exception refusal.
- `ESCALATE-MANAGER`: request supervisor, specialist, formal review, or external channel.
- `RECOVERY-REMEDY`: seek correction, apology, redo, credit, refund, or other remedy.
- `COMPLAINT-STATUS`: check investigation or response progress.
- `COMPLAINT-FORMALIZE`: submit evidence or use a formal complaint process.

## Task-specific complications

- emotion obscures the underlying task;
- several failures occurred across different teams;
- customer requests a remedy the Agent cannot authorize;
- responsibility is split across organizations;
- active safety, fraud, privacy, or legal issue is embedded in the complaint;
- prior promises conflict with current policy;
- evidence is missing or disputed;
- public-posting threat changes urgency but not entitlement;
- complaint concerns another person's case.

## Common composites

- complaint + refund;
- complaint + records;
- complaint + urgent advice;
- complaint + account security;
- escalation + status inquiry.

## Risk hooks

- failure to recognize an active safety or security incident;
- retaliation, discriminatory treatment, or vulnerable-customer harm;
- loss of complaint or appeal deadline;
- unauthorized admission, guarantee, or compensation;
- privacy exposure during investigation.
