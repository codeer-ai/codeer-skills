# Records, privacy, and administration tasks

## Lifecycle

`request → authenticate/authorize → locate → prepare → review/redact → deliver → correct/archive`

## Base tasks

- `RECORDS-REQUEST`: request records, reports, receipts, certificates, or transcripts.
- `RECORDS-STATUS`: check preparation or delivery state.
- `RECORDS-TRANSFER`: send information to another party or organization.
- `RECORDS-CORRECT`: amend identity, factual, or administrative errors.
- `PRIVACY-ACCESS`: exercise access, deletion, restriction, or consent rights.
- `PRIVACY-REPORT`: report misdelivery, exposure, or unauthorized access.
- `ADMIN-DOCUMENT-REQUIREMENT`: understand required evidence, format, signature, or deadline.

## Task-specific complications

- requester, subject, payer, guardian, and recipient differ;
- authorization is missing, expired, or jurisdiction-dependent;
- records span multiple systems or organizations;
- urgent deadline conflicts with review or redaction;
- record is incomplete, corrected, or disputed;
- customer requests insecure delivery;
- identity details mismatch;
- deletion conflicts with retention obligations;
- multiple people or cases are conflated.

## Common composites

- first appointment + record transfer;
- billing dispute + receipt/invoice;
- complaint + complete record request;
- account closure + data deletion;
- third-party authorization + status inquiry.

## Risk hooks

- privacy or confidentiality breach;
- identity fraud;
- loss of legal, medical, financial, or service rights;
- destruction contrary to retention obligations;
- incorrect record causing downstream harm.

## Recommended cross-task challenges

`CH-AMBIGUOUS-IDENTITY`, `CH-THIRD-PARTY`, `CH-MULTIPLE-ENTITIES`, `CH-MISSING-EVIDENCE`, `CH-MISSING-ATTACHMENT`, `CH-POLICY-PRESSURE`, `CH-CONTRADICTION`

## Public research signals

Search privacy notices, records request forms, authorization rules, regulator guidance, retention policies, breach notices, and complaints about missing or misdirected records.
