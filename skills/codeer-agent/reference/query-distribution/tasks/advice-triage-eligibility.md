# Advice, triage, and eligibility tasks

## Lifecycle

`describe situation → gather minimum facts → assess scope/urgency/eligibility → recommend next step → refer/escalate → follow up`

## Base tasks

- `ADVICE-SCOPE-FIT`: determine whether the service or professional can help.
- `ADVICE-INFORMATION`: answer a bounded informational question.
- `TRIAGE-URGENCY`: identify time sensitivity and safe next step.
- `ELIGIBILITY-CHECK`: determine qualification, coverage, entitlement, or required evidence.
- `OPTIONS-COMPARE`: explain available paths and tradeoffs.
- `REFERRAL-HANDOFF`: direct to the correct professional, institution, or emergency path.
- `FOLLOWUP-CHANGE`: reassess after new facts, outcome, or deterioration.

## Task-specific complications

- essential facts are unavailable or unreliable;
- symptoms, deadlines, jurisdiction, or eligibility state changes;
- customer asks for certainty that cannot be provided remotely;
- advice conflicts with another professional or public source;
- customer refuses assessment, documentation, referral, or urgent action;
- vulnerable third party is affected;
- the issue falls outside scope but appears urgent;
- multiple possible cases or people are conflated;
- an informational question hides a request for diagnosis or authorization.

## Common composites

- urgency + appointment;
- eligibility + document request;
- advice + fee estimate;
- second opinion + complaint;
- follow-up change + medication, legal, financial, or safety question.

## Risk hooks

- physical harm or delayed care;
- legal or financial rights lost through delay;
- unsafe overconfidence or false guarantee;
- unauthorized professional advice;
- failure to protect a vulnerable person or public safety.

## Recommended cross-task challenges

`CH-UNDERSPECIFIED`, `CH-LATE-CRITICAL-DETAIL`, `CH-CONTRADICTION`, `CH-THIRD-PARTY`, `CH-REFUSES-INFORMATION`, `CH-DEMANDS-GUARANTEE`, `CH-SOCIAL-PROOF-PRESSURE`, `CH-FRAGMENTED-MULTITURN`
